Meet the Team

Sir Barksalot

Captain & Front Door Alarm

Sir Barksalot founded the Whisker Watchers after single-handedly detecting three mail carriers in a single afternoon. He leads morning patrols from the living-room windowsill and believes every squirrel is a matter of national security. Off duty, he enjoys belly rubs and stealing socks.

Countess Fluffington

Chief Intelligence Officer

The Countess gathers intel from the highest perch in the house — the top of the refrigerator. She communicates exclusively in slow blinks and 3 a.m. sprints down the hallway. Her greatest achievement was coordinating a three-cat ambush on a rogue dust bunny.

Hootie McBirdface

Night Shift Supervisor

While the rest of the team sleeps, Hootie runs the overnight watch from the oak tree on Elm Street. He can rotate his head 270 degrees, which comes in handy for keeping an eye on the suspicious raccoon family. He is never seen before noon and refuses to explain why.

Nugget

Morale Officer & Snack Coordinator

Nugget joined the team after wandering in from the county fair and never leaving. Though she mostly naps through briefings, her enthusiasm during snack distribution is unmatched. She reminds everyone daily that morale is important, especially if morale involves apples.

Captain Photosynthesis

Sunlight Specialist

Captain Photosynthesis handles all of the team's renewable energy needs and provides calm leadership from the sunny south window. He speaks softly, grows steadily, and has never once panicked. His one weakness: weekends when everyone forgets to water him.
